# AUREVA — product vision

## The idea

The plant represents the person behind the screen — their soul, wellbeing and growth. It is not simply a productivity mascot, browser timer or punishment meter.

AUREVA should make caring for the plant feel like caring for the whole person. Digital habits matter, but they are only one part of life.

## The central metaphor

The plant is the main character and the visual centre of the product. The product should make its condition, growth and care actions easy to understand with minimal words and expressive visuals.

## Real life and care

Candidate areas for meaningful plant-care features include:

- Hydration.
- Sleep and rest.
- Movement and physical activity.
- Outdoor time.
- Learning and reading.
- Creativity and hobbies.
- Relationships and connection.
- Reflection and emotional wellbeing.

The product should offer relevant actions, reminders, check-ins or insights for these areas. A feature should not exist merely as an icon or decorative score.

## Digital life

Browsing behaviour should influence points in a clear, context-aware way. Productive/learning activity may earn points; neutral or uncertain activity should not be falsely classified. The system should explain what it observed, what was credited and why.

Distracting browsing can lead to supportive interventions, boundaries or focus prompts. It should not directly injure the plant or erase accumulated growth.

## The outside world

Weather is the real external environment. It is separate from browsing classification and the user's mood. Local weather and environmental conditions may inform practical, gentle recommendations. Weather messages are informational, not a substitute for official alerts.

## Emotional safety

- No shaming language.
- No suggestion that the person is bad or broken because of their habits.
- No loss of accumulated achievements after a difficult day.
- No plant death or punitive plant damage from uncertain browsing classification.
- AUREVA should support users, not diagnose them.

## Experience direction

Colourful, playful, energetic and deeply human. Minimise explanatory prose. Let the plant, weather, browsing status and next action lead the home screen.

## Boundaries

This document captures the product philosophy and desired direction. It is not a claim that every listed wellbeing feature is implemented in the 0.1.4 package.
