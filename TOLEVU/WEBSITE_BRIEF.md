**Working tagline:** Illuminate the Everyday.

## Purpose

Introduce TOLEVU as the parent brand and venture umbrella. The website should communicate the philosophy and provide a clear home for current and future ventures without making unsupported claims about company scale, registration, team size, customers or revenue.

## Brand idea

Technology, nature, luxury, light and enlightenment. The experience should feel elevated, thoughtful, luminous and modern.

## Suggested information architecture

1. **Home** — wordmark, tagline, concise statement of purpose and current featured venture.
2. **About TOLEVU** — what the name represents, the philosophy and the reason for building.
3. **Ventures** — AUREVA as the current documented product; add future ventures only when confirmed.
4. **Insights / Journal** — occasional thinking on technology, wellbeing, creativity and building.
5. **Contact** — a monitored contact method only after one exists.

## Visual requirements

- Preserve the exact stylised brand mark.
- Light, luminous, refined design direction.
- Blend technology and nature without stock “AI company” clichés.
- A distinct colour system is still to be confirmed. Do not treat draft colour mockups as final.
- Use real product imagery and screenshots where possible; do not imply unfinished AUREVA functions are available.

## Items to confirm before launch

- Legal/company description and jurisdiction.
- Official contact email and domain.
- Final logo assets, typography and colour palette.
- Whether “Illuminate the Everyday” remains the final tagline.
- Which ventures are public at launch.
- Privacy policy and terms if the site collects data.
