AUREVA is a product within the TOLEVU ecosystem and the reimagined Digital Greenhouse. Its product personality is colourful, playful, energetic and deeply human. It centres on a plant representing the person behind the screen.

The brands should feel related, but not identical. TOLEVU expresses the parent-level philosophy; AUREVA turns a human-centred wellbeing philosophy into an interactive product.
